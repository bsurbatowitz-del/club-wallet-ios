Privacy policy

Club Wallet for iPhone · last updated 14 October 2026

HomeUser manual & supportPrivacy policy

Summary

The developer of Club Wallet does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you put into the app stays on your iPhone, except what the app sends – at your request – to the services you set up yourself.

What the app stores on your iPhone

  • Your member list (registration number, name, surname, email and, if your list has them, blood type, Rh and photo file names)
  • Member photos, the club logo and background picture you choose
  • Your settings, including your Apple Wallet certificate and private key, your Google Wallet service-account key and your email account password
  • The cards the app creates (in the app's folder in the Files app) and a list of who has already been emailed

This data is kept in the app's private storage, protected by iOS. Deleting the app deletes it. Settings → Reset everything deletes it too.

What the app sends, and to whom

Only when you make or send cards, and only to services you configured:

  • Your email server (for example Gmail): the card email for each member – their name, email address, card picture and Wallet pass.
  • Google Wallet (Google LLC), if you use it: the member's name, registration number, email, season, optional blood type/Rh and photo, to create the member's Google Wallet pass in your Google Wallet issuer account. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Apple Wallet: passes are created and signed on your iPhone. They reach Apple's Wallet only when a member adds the pass.

Nothing is sent to the developer of Club Wallet.

Your members' data

As the club, you are responsible for having your members' permission to make their cards, including any blood type information, and for keeping your member list up to date. Each member's blood type and Rh can be switched off for their card.

Children

Club Wallet is a tool for club administrators and is not directed at children.

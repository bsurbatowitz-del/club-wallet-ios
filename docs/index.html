Club Wallet

Membership cards for Apple Wallet and Google Wallet – made from your member list and emailed to every member.

HomeUser manual & supportPrivacy policy

What it does

  • Imports your member list (.ods, .xlsx or .csv)
  • Designs the card: club name, season, logo, background picture, colours, member photos
  • Makes signed Apple Wallet passes and Google Wallet links, with a QR code holding the member's details
  • Emails each member their card from your own email account
  • Optional blood type and Rh, switchable per member

Get started

Install Club Wallet from the App Store, then follow the user manual. Want to look around first? Open Settings → Load sample club.
